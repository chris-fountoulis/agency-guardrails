# Website care plan for local shops

A website care plan keeps a small business site updated, backed up and fixed after launch, so the owner never has
to think about plugins, security patches or broken forms. Ours covers updates, daily backups and fixes, and the
team replies to requests within one business day.

Our website care plan costs 49 EUR per month (60.76 EUR incl. VAT).
