Bakery website speed: the complete guide

Good bakery website speed means the menu, opening hours and order button appear in under two seconds on a mid-range phone. Most visitors decide within that window whether to stay. Compress photos, serve them in modern formats, cache every page and keep third-party scripts to a minimum, and a small bakery site will load faster than most of its competitors.
